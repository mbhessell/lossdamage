Before you start

Report a loss or damage claim

Use this form to document shipment loss or damage at time of delivery. It takes about 5–10 minutes, and works for one product or several. Both the carrier representative and the FEB employee can sign at the end, in the same sitting — no separate e-signature step needed.

Step 1

Shipment Information

Basic facts about the shipment this claim is for.

Step 2

Products

Add every product involved in this claim. You can add as many as you need.

Please fill in Supplier, Product, and Quantity for each product before continuing.

Step 3

Description & Labor

Describe what happened to the shipment.

Step 4

Photos & Documents

Add the Bill of Lading (BOL) and photos of the damaged/short product. Photos, PDFs, and iPhone (HEIC) photos are all supported.

or drag files here

Step 5

Carrier & Employee

Who's completing this claim, and who's representing the carrier (if present).

All three carrier fields are optional — it's fine to submit if the driver already left before any of this could be captured.
